Sutherland Labs · Conversational AI for customer service

Customers want fast answers. Businesses want lower costs. Nobody wants to wait six months for a chatbot.

  • Head of Design, Conversational AI
  • 2020–2023
  • Team of 5

Sutherland Labs built chatbots that took routine questions off tier-one agents and let customers help themselves, for clients including CBS, Sony PlayStation, Micron, Disney, ESPN, and Mars. I led design for the practice, with a team of designers and conversational writers.

The opportunity

The bots worked. The process behind them didn’t scale. Every client started from scratch: its own discovery, its own flows, its own look. A launch took about six months and was hard to repeat.

What we built

  • A map of the whole lifecycle. I led a deep blueprint analysis of how a bot got made, from sales and discovery through design, build, and launch, to find where every project was reinventing the same work.
  • Patterns for conversation. A library of the moves every bot needs, so writers and designers could build consistent flows for any client instead of starting from a blank page.
  • A design language built to flex. One visual system that each client could make its own, customized at the surface and shared underneath.

The result

A repeatable program that took a new client from kickoff to launch in weeks instead of six months. Most of what was left was scheduling.
