Hired · A talent marketplace for tech jobs

Finding a job is hard. Hiring for one is too. We tried to make both a little less painful.

  • Head of Design
  • 2018–2020
  • Team of 8

Hired turned the job search around: companies applied to candidates, with salary up front. I led design and research across product and marketing, with a team of designers and researchers.

The opportunity

Both sides of hiring run on weak signals. A résumé says little about what someone can do, and a job post says little about what the job is. Everyone wastes time guessing.

The problems worth solving

  • Showing what someone can actually do. Coding challenges let candidates prove their skills instead of listing them.
  • Giving recruiters a better signal. Tools to find best-fit candidates fast, without wading through everyone.
  • Growing beyond the match. We grew the marketplace with new products for employers: coding challenges, data and reporting on hiring, and tools for enterprise teams.

Raising the bar

I set up a research-driven way of working across product and marketing, so decisions started with what candidates and recruiters actually did. My team also tuned and codified the Hired brand, then took it bigger: more awareness, and executed across every surface.
